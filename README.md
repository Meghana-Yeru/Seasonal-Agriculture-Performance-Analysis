# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## VOIS AICTE Aug Batch 2026–2027 – Major Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing agricultural performance across Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons using farm-level data. It examines factors such as crop yield, production, environmental conditions, irrigation, resource usage, water efficiency, disease/pest risk, and profitability. Using Python, statistical analysis, and data visualization, the project identifies important seasonal and regional patterns to support better agricultural planning and data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Clean and prepare agricultural data
- Compare seasonal and regional performance
- Analyze environmental, resource, and economic factors
- Identify patterns and relationships
- Generate data-driven insights and recommendations

## 📊 Dataset

- **Records:** 4,000
- **Variables:** 28
- **Seasons:** Kharif, Rabi, Zaid

The dataset includes farm details, crops, environmental conditions, soil, irrigation, agricultural inputs, yield, production, water usage, profitability, and disease/pest risk.

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Loading
The agricultural dataset is loaded using Pandas.

### 2. Data Understanding
The dataset structure, dimensions, column names, data types, and summary statistics are examined.

### 3. Data Cleaning
- Missing values are identified and handled.
- Duplicate records are checked.
- Data types are verified.
- Potential outliers are investigated.

### 4. Exploratory Data Analysis

#### Univariate Analysis
Analysis of individual variables such as:
- Season
- Crop
- Yield
- Profit
- Rainfall
- Other numerical variables

#### Bivariate Analysis
Analysis of relationships between two variables such as:
- Season and Yield
- Season and Profit
- Rainfall and Yield
- Farm Area and Production
- Irrigation Method and Yield

#### Multivariate Analysis
Multiple variables are analyzed together to understand:
- Crop and seasonal performance
- Environmental conditions and agricultural outcomes
- Yield and profit relationships
- Seasonal variations across different factors

State-wise yield analysis, Financial risk analysis, Disease/pest risk and yield analysis

## 💡 Key Insights

- Agricultural performance varies across different seasons.
- Kharif shows comparatively better yield and profitability.
- Zaid shows comparatively weaker economic performance.
- Zaid has higher water usage and lower water-use efficiency.
- Disease and pest risk is comparatively higher during Kharif.
- Water efficiency has a strong positive relationship with crop yield.
- Disease/pest risk shows little linear relationship with crop yield.
- Crop yield varies across different states.
- Crop performance differs across seasons, highlighting the importance of seasonal planning.
- Agricultural performance depends on multiple factors rather than a single variable.
